256 × 256 spherical memory window. Acoustic tests, manual APL capability exchange, and a hardware-agnostic native-language Q&A laboratory. Morse frame transfer is experimental; FSK packet transfer and autonomous transport switching are not yet implemented.

Drag to rotate the sphere. Each cell stores a full 0–255 byte value; validity is tracked separately. This prototype does not transmit or receive audio.
